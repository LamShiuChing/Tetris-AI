Versus basics

  • Clearing lines sends garbage to your opponent; it rises from the bottom of their board.
  • Incoming garbage fills the meter beside your board. Clearing lines first cancels it.
  • T-spins and quads hit hardest. Do them back-to-back (B2B) for a bonus.
  • Clears in a row build a combo (REN) for extra garbage.
  • Top out and you're K.O.
